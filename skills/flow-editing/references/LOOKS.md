## Which look for which job
Night cars: furys-chain (dark, strong lamps). Street or travel: furys-still. Event recap that has to stay tasteful: izxa-light with about 4 hot and 8 black frames per 10 s, and none on speeches or children. Music visualizer: izxa-light, 18 fps, 4:3. Fashion or product: shape match and hot landings. Daylight, talking, wide locked-off footage: flow is the wrong tool, unless you grade it to night with one colour left (see examples in the README).
